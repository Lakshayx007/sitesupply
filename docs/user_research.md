# User research plan

> **This is a template.** The findings below are empty on purpose. Fill them only with real conversations.

## Goals
1. Learn how site engineers find out a delivery is late today, and how long that takes.
2. Learn what a late delivery actually costs them (crew time, equipment, wasted loads), in their own words and numbers.
3. Learn what they do in the first 15 minutes after a truck is late, and who they call.
4. Check whether an early, explained warning would change what they do.

## Who to talk to
Five people: three site engineers, one contractor, one procurement manager. Different cities if possible.

## Questions (Mom Test style)
Ask about the past, not about the product. Don't pitch. Don't ask "would you use an app?"

1. Tell me about the last delivery that went wrong. What happened?
2. When did you find out? How?
3. What did you do next? Who did you call?
4. What did it cost you? Crew hours, equipment, materials, the schedule?
5. How often does this happen in a month?
6. How do you choose which vendor gets an order?
7. Do you track deliveries anywhere today? Show me.
8. Was there anything you saw earlier that, looking back, told you it would be late?
9. Last time this happened, what did you wish you had known, and when?
